## 🌟 Welcome!

This app helps you manage Christian Union attendance and send messages to members. It's easy to use - let's get started!

---

## 🎯 Getting Started

### Step 1: Install the App
1. Get the **app-release.apk** file from your admin
2. Open it on your phone
3. Tap **"Install"**
4. If it says "Blocked", go to Settings and allow "Unknown Sources"

### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Register"** (bottom of screen)
3. Fill in:
   - Your full name
   - Your email
   - A strong password (at least 8 characters)
4. Tap **"Register"**
5. Wait for admin to approve you (you'll get a notification)

### Step 3: Sign In
1. Open the app
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap **"Sign In"**
4. Welcome! 🎉

## ✍️ How to Register Attendees

### Method 1: Manual Entry (Slow but Accurate)

1. Tap **"Registration"** on home screen
2. Tap **"Add Attendee"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   ```
   Name: John Doe
   Phone: 0712345678
   Year of Study: 3rd Year (or leave blank for non-students)
   Location: Main Campus
   Category: Student (or Associate/Visitor)
   ```
4. Tap **"Save"**
5. Attendee is added! ✅

**Tips:**
- Phone numbers must start with 0 (e.g., 0712345678)
- Use correct format or it won't save
- Year of Study is optional for Associates and Visitors

### Method 2: Scan Document (Fast! 🚀)

1. Tap **"Registration"** on home screen
2. Tap the **camera icon** (top right)
3. Point camera at attendance sheet
4. Take a clear photo
5. App extracts names and phone numbers automatically
6. Review the list - fix any errors
7. Tap **"Save All"**
8. All attendees added at once! 🎉

**Tips:**
- Use good lighting
- Keep paper flat and straight
- Make sure handwriting is clear
- Review before saving - AI isn't perfect!

### Method 3: Import from File

1. Tap **"Registration"** on home screen
2. Tap **"Import"** button
3. Select your CSV or Excel file
4. Map the columns (Name → Name, Phone → Phone, etc.)
5. Tap **"Import"**
6. All attendees imported! 📥

## 💬 How to Send Messages

### Quick Message (Send to Everyone)

1. Tap **"Messaging"** on home screen
2. Type your message in the box
3. Tap **"Send to All"**
4. Confirm
5. Messages sent! 📤

### Filtered Message (Send to Specific Group)

1. Tap **"Messaging"** on home screen
2. Tap **"Filter Recipients"**
3. Choose filters:
   - **By Category:** Students only, Associates only, etc.
   - **By Location:** Main Campus, Hostels, etc.
   - **By Service:** Attendees of a specific service
   - **By Year:** 1st years, 2nd years, etc.
4. See how many people will receive it
5. Type your message
6. Tap **"Send"**
7. Done! 🎊

**Message Tips:**
- Keep messages short (160 characters = 1 SMS)
- Be clear and friendly
- Include important details (date, time, location)
- Preview before sending

**Example Messages:**
```
Hi! CU meeting this Friday 5pm at Main Hall. 
Topic: Faith & Studies. See you there! 🙏

Reminder: Tomorrow's service starts at 10am. 
Bring a friend! God bless 💙

Thank you for attending today's service! 
Next week: Special guest speaker. Don't miss it!
```

## ⚙️ Settings & Profile

### Update Your Profile

1. Tap **"Settings"** on home screen
2. Tap **"Profile"**
3. Update:
   - Your name
   - Your email
   - Your password
   - Your photo (optional)
4. Tap **"Save"**

### Backup Your Data

**Important! Do this regularly!**

1. Tap **"Settings"** on home screen
2. Tap **"Backup & Restore"**
3. Tap **"Backup Now"**
4. Data is saved to cloud ☁️
5. You'll see "Backup successful!"

### Restore Data

If you lose data or get a new phone:

1. Install the app
2. Sign in with your account
3. Tap **"Settings"** → **"Backup & Restore"**
4. Tap **"Restore from Backup"**
5. Choose which backup to restore
6. Tap **"Restore"**
7. All your data is back! 🎉

## 🔄 Syncing & Offline Mode

### How Syncing Works

- **Online:** Changes sync automatically to cloud
- **Offline:** Changes saved locally on your phone
- **Back Online:** Everything syncs automatically

### Manual Sync

1. Pull down on any screen (swipe down)
2. See "Syncing..." message
3. Wait for it to finish
4. You're up to date! ✅

### Check Sync Status

Look at the top of the screen:
- **Green cloud ☁️** - Everything synced
- **Orange cloud 🟠** - Syncing now...
- **Red cloud 🔴** - Sync failed (check internet)

## 🆘 Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem: Can't Sign In
**Solutions:**
1. Check your email and password are correct
2. Make sure you're approved (ask admin)
3. Check internet connection
4. Try "Forgot Password" to reset

### Problem: Can't Register Attendee
**Solutions:**
1. Check phone number format (must start with 0)
2. Make sure all required fields are filled
3. Check internet connection
4. Try again in a few minutes

### Problem: Messages Not Sending
**Solutions:**
1. Check phone has SMS permission
2. Make sure you have airtime/SMS bundle
3. Check internet connection
4. Try sending to fewer people at once

### Problem: App is Slow
**Solutions:**
1. Close other apps
2. Restart your phone
3. Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → TUK CU → Clear Cache
4. Update to latest version

### Problem: Data Not Showing
**Solutions:**
1. Pull down to refresh
2. Check internet connection
3. Sign out and sign in again
4. Contact admin
