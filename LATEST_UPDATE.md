## 📱 How to Use

### Access Your 128 Contacts:
1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Contacts"** tab (second icon from left)
3. See all your contacts instantly!

### Send Message to All:
1. Go to Contacts tab
2. (Optional) Apply filters
3. Tap **"Message All"** button
4. Type your message
5. Tap **"Send"**
6. Done! ✅

### Filter Contacts:
1. Tap **"Location"** chip → Select location
2. Tap **"Year"** chip → Select year
3. Tap **"Category"** chip → Select category
4. See filtered results instantly
5. Tap **"Clear"** to remove filters

### Search for Someone:
1. Type name or phone in search bar
2. Results appear instantly
3. Tap message icon to send SMS

## 🔒 Data Safety

Your 128 contacts are safe in:
1. **Local Database** - On your phone
2. **Will sync to Firestore** - When online
3. **Never deleted** - Even when session cleared
4. **Survives reinstall** - Data persists
